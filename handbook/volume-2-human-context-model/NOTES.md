# Volume 2 — Parking Lot

Ideas already committed to this volume, ahead of drafting its chapter
outline. Not chapters yet — just commitments that must not get lost when
that outline gets written.

## History as a laboratory
Historical figures are not the end goal — they're the safest place to
validate the system before it ever touches a living person's data: no
consent issues, abundant secondary sourcing, and errors are cheap to catch.
Needs a dedicated section explaining this as a deliberate methodological
choice, not a scoping compromise. Should connect directly to the Evidence
Hierarchy (Volume 1, Ch. 6) — history is where Level 1–3 evidence is most
plentiful relative to Level 4 inference.

This is the *strategy*; it is not the *motivation*. See
`../founding-problem.md` — the actual reason this exists is family memory,
not historical figures. Say this plainly rather than letting the reader
assume history is the end goal: public figures are the safe rehearsal for
a system ultimately meant to be trusted with someone's own grandfather.

## Technology as first-class context
Not an example nested under "Environment." Its own node in the
causal-forces spine, on equal footing with Family, Education, Economy,
Culture. The question "what tools, discoveries, and constraints existed"
is answerable independently of "what did the economy or culture look
like," and collapsing it into Environment would lose that resolution.
